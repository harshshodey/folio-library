Literary excerpts in dist/passages.js are reproduced from historical works by
Lewis Carroll, Jane Austen, Oscar Wilde, Henry David Thoreau, Mark Twain, and
Robert Louis Stevenson. These are public-domain works, not newly authored
Folio text. Author, work, location, source URL, and editorial context are
provided in the app and SOURCES.txt. No ownership of these works is claimed.

DM Sans and Manrope are requested from Google Fonts at runtime. Font files
are not distributed in this repository. They have their own licenses; see
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/dmsans and
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope .

Book titles and author names identify the sample catalog. The cover mockups
are CSS illustrations, not scans of commercially published book covers.
